## Which track applies

| Your use | Track |
|---|---|
| Personal use, study, research, evaluation | 1 — AGPL-3.0 |
| Internal deployment of an unmodified build | 1 — AGPL-3.0 |
| Modified build, deployed or distributed with its source disclosed | 1 — AGPL-3.0 |
| Closed-source hosted service offered to others | 2 — commercial |
| Embedded in a proprietary product that you ship | 2 — commercial |

Commercial use itself is allowed under **both** tracks. What distinguishes them is the source-disclosure obligation: AGPL-3.0 §13 requires that users of your modified build — including users of a network service — receive the corresponding source.
